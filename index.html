207 segments from sztanko's original Zagreb map, computed for 21 Dec (Zagreb only). On Dec solstice at ±3° your highlights should sit on top of these.
Why 21 Jun ≠ 21 Dec. The original map only used the December solstice. Horizon refraction makes the two solstices' folded orientations differ by ~2°, so on 21 Jun many of his streets (e.g. around Jarun) sit just outside ±3° and need ~3.7°. 137/207 align on both solstices; 67 are December-only.
Sun azimuths via an embedded solar-position routine (USNO low-precision), reproducing the original solsticestreets pyephem values to ~0.1°. Street data © OpenStreetMap contributors, fetched live via Overpass.
